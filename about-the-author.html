About the Author

Will Hardwick is a writer known for his candid exploration of identity, sexuality, and the complexities of modern relationships. With a background in psychology and journalism, Will draws from personal experiences and keen observations to craft stories that resonate with readers on a profound level. His novels delve into the tumultuous journey of self-discovery, challenging societal norms while celebrating authentic connections: a captivating narrative that shines a light on the often-hidden struggles of love and acceptance. The books explore historical themes that reverberate across the centuries: the importance of love, sex, and belonging that never changes.

Born and raised in the vibrant city of San Francisco, Will has always been inspired by the diverse experiences of the LGBTQ+ community. He currently resides in Los Angeles, where he spends his days writing and advocating for mental health awareness and LGBTQ+ rights. When not penning his next novel, Will can be found hiking through California's breathtaking landscapes or engaging in spirited conversations over a good cup of coffee, both of which inspired Forbidden Trails. Or just staying at home, waiting for the mailman...
