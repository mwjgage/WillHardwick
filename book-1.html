Highland Romance

Return to Glenmor

Ten years after she left the glen without saying goodbye, Isla Faircross is back — not to stay, she tells herself, just long enough to settle her grandmother's cottage and go. But Glenmor has a way of pulling people home, and so does Rory Kincade, the boy she once loved, now a man running the estate she was raised on.

Neither of them planned on this. Not the old arguments that resurface in new forms, not the long walks that turn into long talks, not the quiet realization that the life Isla built elsewhere was never quite the one she wanted.

A slow-burn second-chance romance about the places — and people — worth returning to.
