Highland Romance

Hiking Home

Content creator Maggie Doyle signs up for a week-long trek through the Highlands expecting good footage and better views. She does not expect her guide to be Callum Reid — infuriatingly capable, allergic to small talk, and the reason her carefully planned itinerary keeps falling apart.

Mile by mile, the distance between them narrows, and Maggie starts to wonder whether the story she came to tell is really the one worth telling.

A warm, outdoorsy enemies-to-lovers romance set against Scotland's wildest scenery.
