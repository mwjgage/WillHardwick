Western Romance

Silver Creek

When rancher's daughter Eleanor Hayes inherits a debt she never knew existed, the only man willing to help is the one her family ran out of town seven years ago. Jesse Calder has spent those years building a name for himself; he did not plan on spending any of them back in Silver Creek.

But a shared threat to the land they both love forces old wounds into the open — and an old, unfinished feeling right along with them.

A dusty-trail, big-hearted Western romance about second chances earned the hard way.
