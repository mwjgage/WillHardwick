Cozy Mystery

Murder at the Manor House

When the village's least-liked landowner is found dead in his own library, amateur sleuth and part-time florist Evelyn Marsh finds herself with a shop full of suspects — half the village had a reason to want him gone, and all of them are her customers.

Between flower orders and awkward interviews with the local inspector, Evelyn untangles a knot of old grudges, older secrets, and one very well-hidden motive.

A gentle, puzzle-driven mystery with a cast of characters readers will want to visit again.
