Holy orders, unholy desires.

Dirty Habits

A sizzling tale of forbidden passion in the shadows of medieval monasticism.

In the sacred halls of a 12th-century abbey, Brother Aldric's vow of chastity shatters when Brother Thomas arrives with his skilled hands and knowing eyes. As they collaborate on illuminated manuscripts by day, their bodies create even more beautiful art by night.

What begins as furtive touches in the scriptorium escalates to desperate couplings against ancient stone walls. Their passion spills across parchment in secret illustrations that merge the sacred and profane, leaving traces of their forbidden desire hidden in plain sight.

When the zealous Brother Benedict threatens to expose their sinful romance, Aldric and Thomas must navigate a dangerous game of power, lust, and revenge. Their clandestine affair—witnessed through keyholes, joined by other desperate monks, and consummated with sacred relics—tests their commitment to each other. From a monk's private confession comes a story of craft, craving, and peril: Aldric risks everything—his art, his vows, his life—to keep the man who taught him how to feel.

Spanning decades of raw desire and culminating in a love that transcends even death, this explicit chronicle of medieval queer passion burns with the intensity of a thousand church candles.

Content note: Contains explicit M, MM, and MMM scenes, religious blasphemy, and the creative use of holy relics.
