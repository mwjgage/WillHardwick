Seven Day’s Hard Work

Public places. Private pleasure. Pure obsession.

Public places. Private pleasure. Pure obsession. Mark has one rule: never get caught. He has one addiction: the risk of getting caught. For twenty-eight-year-old graphic designer Mark, restraint isn't in his vocabulary. His days revolve around finding moments of release in places he absolutely shouldn't—park benches at dusk, gym saunas mid-morning, packed Tube carriages during rush hour, fitting rooms in crowded shops.

The thrill isn't just the act itself—it's knowing that at any second, someone could look his way. When an anonymous forum user calls him a liar and challenges him to prove his exhibitionist confessions are real, Mark decides to document seven consecutive days of increasingly reckless encounters. No safety net. No second thoughts. Just his phone camera and a week of dangerous proof.

Seven days. Seven encounters. Seven chances to get caught. Mark's about to discover his exhibitionism wasn't as anonymous as he thought—and his biggest fantasy might not be getting caught, but being watched by someone who's been paying very close attention.

Content warning

Contains explicit scenes of M, MM, and MMM public behaviour, exhibitionism, voyeurism, and graphic gay content. This is unapologetically provocative erotica for readers who like their tension high and their characters shameless.
