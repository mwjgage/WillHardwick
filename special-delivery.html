When discretion meets desire, the postman always comes twice.

Jamie Fletcher has everyone in the sleepy village of Millfield fooled. By day, he's the pristine town councilman with the perfect lawn. By night, he's a desperate man with a collection of silicone toys that would make a porn star blush.

For a year, Jamie's been satisfying his needs with synthetic substitutes, ordered online and delivered straight to his door. The only human connection in his carefully compartmentalized life? Those brief, electric moments when Mike—the muscular postman with forearms that could crush a man's resolve—hands over his discreetly packaged “office supplies.”

When a torn package reveals Jamie's secret to Mike, their delivery interactions become charged with knowing glances and lingering touches. Jamie can't stop ordering more toys, scheduling more deliveries, craving those fleeting moments of connection.

But when a thunderstorm leaves Mike drenched on Jamie's doorstep, an invitation to come inside and dry off changes everything. As lightning cracks outside, inhibitions fall away inside—and Jamie discovers that Mike has been harboring fantasies of his own about what happens to those packages after delivery.

Suddenly, Jamie's impressive toy collection has a new purpose: education. As town councilman becomes teacher and mailman becomes willing student, they explore pleasure together, pushing boundaries and discovering that the most satisfying package of all might be the one in Mike's uniform shorts.

Special Delivery is a scorching hot, filthy gay erotic novel about finding connection in the most unexpected places—and discovering that sometimes, the real thing is better than anything that comes in the mail.
